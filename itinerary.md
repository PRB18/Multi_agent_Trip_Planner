### 1. Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Group Size:** 10 people
**Accommodation:** Shared Airbnb Villa (2 nights)
**Local Transport:** 12-seater Tempo Traveller
**Meals:** Purely Vegetarian

**Day 1: Mumbai to Goa – Overnight Train Journey**

*   **18:00 - 19:00:** Assemble at Mumbai's Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (LTT) or Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) as per train schedule.
*   **19:00 - 20:00:** Board your designated Sleeper Class (SL) train to Madgaon (MAO), Goa.
*   **20:00 onwards:** Enjoy a packed vegetarian dinner from home or purchase simple vegetarian meals/snacks available on the train. Settle in for the overnight journey.

**Day 2: South Goa Exploration & Beach Bliss**

*   **07:00 - 08:00:** Arrive at Madgaon (MAO) railway station.
*   **08:00 - 08:30:** Meet your pre-booked 12-seater Tempo Traveller outside the station.
*   **08:30 - 09:30:** Drive to your Airbnb villa located in South Goa (e.g., near Palolem/Agonda).
*   **09:30 - 10:30:** Check-in to the villa, freshen up, and have a light vegetarian breakfast at a local eatery or at the villa (if self-catering).
*   **10:30 - 13:00:** Head to **Palolem Beach**, known for its crescent shape and calm waters. Relax, take a stroll, or simply enjoy the serene environment.
*   **13:00 - 14:00:** Enjoy a delicious vegetarian lunch at one of the many beach shacks in Palolem.
*   **14:00 - 16:00:** Visit **Agonda Beach**, a quieter and more pristine beach, perfect for relaxation and soaking in the sun.
*   **16:00 - 18:00:** Drive to **Cabo de Rama Fort** for historical exploration and breathtaking panoramic views of the Arabian Sea, ideal for sunset photography.
*   **18:00 - 19:30:** Return to your Airbnb villa.
*   **19:30 onwards:** Freshen up and relax.
*   **20:30 onwards:** Indulge in a vegetarian dinner at a local restaurant in South Goa, experiencing authentic Goan flavors.

**Day 3: North Goa Vibes & Return Journey**

*   **08:00 - 09:00:** Enjoy a vegetarian breakfast at your Airbnb villa or a nearby eatery.
*   **09:00 - 10:00:** Check out from your Airbnb villa. Load all luggage into the Tempo Traveller.
*   **10:00 - 12:00:** Begin your scenic drive from South Goa (Palolem/Agonda area) towards North Goa (Anjuna/Vagator area). This transit is a one-way journey for the day, adhering to the "avoid cross-region travel on the same day" constraint.
*   **12:00 - 13:30:** Visit **Chapora Fort**, famous for its iconic views over Vagator Beach and the Chapora River.
*   **13:30 - 14:30:** Have a vegetarian lunch at a popular restaurant near Vagator or Anjuna.
*   **14:30 - 16:30:** Explore **Vagator Beach** and **Anjuna Beach**. Enjoy the vibrant atmosphere, walk along the shore, and perhaps browse local stalls (note: the famous Anjuna Flea Market is seasonal, typically Nov-Apr).
*   **16:30 - 17:30:** Drive to **Thivim (THVM) railway station**, which is conveniently located for North Goa departures.
*   **17:30 - 18:30:** Arrive at Thivim station, collect your tickets, and wait for your train.
*   **19:00 - 20:00:** Board your Sleeper Class (SL) train back to Mumbai.
*   **20:00 onwards:** Enjoy a packed vegetarian dinner from a local eatery or simple vegetarian meals/snacks on the train.

**Day 4: Arrival in Mumbai**

*   **07:00 - 08:00:** Arrive back at Mumbai's LTT/CSMT station.
*   **08:00 onwards:** Have a vegetarian breakfast on the train (packed or purchased).
*   The trip concludes as you disembark in Mumbai.
